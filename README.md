# Chess Verse Lite

Chess Verse Lite is a polished chess game review experience that helps players understand their games, revisit important decisions, and practice better alternatives.

[Open Chess Verse Lite](https://chess-verse-lite.vercel.app/)

## What You Can Do

### Review Complete Games

Load a PGN and move through the game at your own pace. Use the board controls, arrow keys, or move history to revisit every position without losing your place.

### Understand Every Move

Chess Verse Lite evaluates the game and presents:

- White and Black accuracy
- Move classifications such as Best, Good, Inaccuracy, Mistake, Miss, and Blunder
- Position evaluations
- Better-move suggestions
- Recommended continuations
- A complete, clickable move history

### Learn With AI Coach

After a game has been analyzed, AI Coach turns the results into a concise, easy-to-understand review containing:

- An overall game summary
- Strengths demonstrated during the game
- Areas to improve
- Important critical moments
- Practical training recommendations
- Opening, middlegame, and endgame observations when supported by the game
- A clear one-line takeaway

### Explore in Practice Mode

Switch to Practice Mode from any reviewed position to try different ideas directly on the board. Click a piece to see its legal moves, click a highlighted square to play, or use drag and drop.

Practice Mode also supports:

- Captures and special moves
- Promotion choices
- Position evaluation after each move
- Suggested moves and continuations
- Backward and forward navigation through your practice line
- Resetting to the original reviewed position

### Return to Recent Games

Successfully analyzed games appear in the Recent Games section of the Load PGN window. Select a previous game to restore its PGN quickly, or clear the history whenever you choose.

## Designed for Comfortable Review

- Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts
- Large, clear chessboard with an evaluation bar
- Separate Moves, Info, and AI Coach tabs
- Contained scrolling for long reports and move histories
- Board flipping and optional move sounds
- Keyboard-friendly controls and visible focus states
- Dark glass-inspired visual design
- Clear loading, progress, cancellation, empty, and error states

## Typical Workflow

1. Select **Load PGN** and paste a chess game.
2. Start the analysis and follow its progress in the Moves tab.
3. Review accuracy, classifications, suggested moves, and critical positions.
4. Open AI Coach for a compact coaching report.
5. Switch to Practice Mode to explore better alternatives on the board.

Chess Verse Lite keeps the focus on the game: review what happened, understand why it mattered, and immediately practice a better approach.
